01

Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Tibetan Scientific Calculator mobile application. It explains how information is handled when you use the app.

02

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information or usage data. The app does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal details.

03

Calculations and history

Your calculations, memory values, and calculation history are processed locally on your device. They are not sent to us or to any third party. Calculation history is kept only for the current app session and can be cleared from within the app.

04

Third-party services

The app does not contain advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, or third-party data-collection SDKs. It does not require an account or an internet connection to perform calculations.

05

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users. Because the app does not collect personal data, no personal information from children is stored or shared by us.

06

Changes to this policy

If the app's privacy practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes take effect. The effective date at the top of this page will also be revised.

07

Contact us

If you have a privacy question or need support, please email us. We will respond as soon as reasonably possible.
